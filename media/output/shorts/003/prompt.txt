TITLE: Luke 1:1-12 — Zacharias in the Temple
PROMPT: Many have sought to tell the story of what has happened among us. Those who witnessed these things firsthand passed them on, and now I too have traced their course carefully, writing this for you, most excellent Theophilus, so that you may be certain of the truths you have been taught. In the days of Herod, king of Judea, there lived a priest named Zacharias, from the priestly division of Abijah. His wife was Elizabeth, a daughter of Aaron. Both were righteous before God, living blamelessly according to all the commandments and ordinances of the Lord. Yet they had no child, for Elizabeth was barren, and both were well advanced in years. One day, while Zacharias was serving in the temple as his priestly duty required, he drew lots and was chosen to enter the temple and burn incense. At that hour, the people outside were praying. Then an angel of the Lord appeared to him, standing on the right side of the altar of incense. When Zacharias saw him, he was filled with fear and trembling.
SUMMARY: Zacharias, a righteous priest, is chosen to burn incense in the temple when an angel suddenly appears to him.
