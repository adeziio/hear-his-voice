TITLE: Mark 1:12-20 — Jesus Tempted in the Wilderness and Calls His First Disciples
PROMPT: Immediately, the Spirit drove him into the wilderness. He was there for forty days, tempted by Satan, and he was with the wild animals; the angels were serving him. After John was taken into custody, Jesus came into Galilee, preaching the Good News of God’s Kingdom, and saying, “The time is fulfilled, and God’s Kingdom is at hand! Repent, and believe in the Good News.” Passing along by the Sea of Galilee, he saw Simon and Andrew, the brother of Simon, casting a net into the sea, for they were fishermen. Jesus said to them, “Come after me, and I will make you into fishers for men.” Immediately they left their nets and followed him. Going on a little further from there, he saw James the son of Zebedee and John his brother, who were also in the boat mending the nets. Immediately he called them, and they left their father, Zebedee, in the boat with the hired servants, and went after him.
SUMMARY: Jesus is tempted by Satan in the wilderness for forty days and later calls Simon, Andrew, James, and John to follow him as disciples.
