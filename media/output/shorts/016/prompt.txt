TITLE: John 1:43-51 — Jesus Calls Philip and Nathanael
PROMPT: The next day, Jesus set his heart on going to Galilee. There, he found Philip and said simply, “Follow me.” Philip was from Bethsaida, the hometown of Andrew and Peter. He went looking for Nathanael and told him, “We have found him of whom Moses in the law and also the prophets wrote: Jesus of Nazareth, the son of Joseph.” Nathanael scoffed, asking, “Can any good thing come out of Nazareth?” Philip replied, “Come and see.” As Nathanael approached, Jesus saw him coming and said, “Behold, an Israelite indeed, in whom is no deceit!” Startled, Nathanael asked, “How do you know me?” Jesus answered, “Before Philip called you, when you were under the fig tree, I saw you.” Amazed, Nathanael declared, “Rabbi, you are the Son of God! You are King of Israel!” Jesus said to him, “Because I told you, ‘I saw you underneath the fig tree,’ do you believe? You will see greater things than these!” He continued, “Most certainly, I tell you all, hereafter you will see heaven opened, and the angels of God ascending and descending on the Son of Man.”
SUMMARY: Jesus calls Philip to follow him, and through Philip’s invitation, Nathanael is brought to Jesus, where he recognizes Jesus as the Son of God.
