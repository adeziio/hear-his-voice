TITLE: Mark 1:21-31 — Jesus Teaches in Capernaum Synagogue
PROMPT: In Capernaum, on the Sabbath day, Jesus entered a synagogue and began to teach. Those listening were astonished, for he taught with authority, unlike the scribes. At once, a man in their midst cried out, “What do we have to do with you, Jesus, you Nazarene? Have you come to destroy us? I know who you are: the Holy One of God!” Jesus looked at him and said, “Be quiet, and come out of him!” With a violent cry, the unclean spirit convulsed the man and then left him. All were amazed, whispering among themselves, “What is this new doctrine? He commands even the unclean spirits, and they obey him!” The news spread quickly throughout Galilee and beyond. Leaving the synagogue, Jesus went to the house of Simon and Andrew, where James and John were also present. There, Simon’s wife’s mother lay ill with a fever. They told Jesus about her, and he came to her, took her by the hand, and raised her up. Immediately, the fever left her, and she rose to serve them.
SUMMARY: Jesus teaches with authority in the synagogue, casts out a demon, and heals Simon’s mother-in-law.
