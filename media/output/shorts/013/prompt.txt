TITLE: Matthew 2:10-15 — Wise Men Worship and Joseph Flees to Egypt
PROMPT: When they saw the star, the wise men were filled with great joy. They went to Bethlehem and found the young child with Mary his mother. Falling to their knees, they worshiped him. From their treasures they gave gifts of gold, frankincense, and myrrh. But then, in a dream, they were warned not to return to Herod. So they left by another route, returning to their own country. Soon after, an angel of the Lord appeared to Joseph in a dream. The angel said, 'Arise and take the young child and his mother, and flee into Egypt, and stay there until I tell you, for Herod will seek the young child to destroy him.' Joseph obeyed immediately. By night he took the child and Mary and went to Egypt. He remained there until the death of Herod, so that what had been spoken by the Lord through the prophet might be fulfilled: 'Out of Egypt I called my son.'
SUMMARY: The wise men worship Jesus and offer gifts, while Joseph takes Mary and Jesus to Egypt to escape Herod's threat, fulfilling prophecy.
