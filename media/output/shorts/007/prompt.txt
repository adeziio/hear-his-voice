TITLE: Luke 1:13-17 — The Announcement of John’s Birth
PROMPT: Zacharias stood in silence, his heart heavy with doubt. But suddenly, a voice spoke from within the temple — not of man, but of God. “Don’t be afraid, Zacharias,” the angel said. “Your request has been heard. Your wife, Elizabeth, will bear you a son, and you shall call his name John.” A son — a promise that would bring joy to Zacharias and Elizabeth after so many years of waiting. Joy and gladness would fill their home, and many would rejoice at his birth. For this child would be great in the sight of the Lord. He would drink no wine nor strong drink. From the very womb, he would be filled with the Holy Spirit. He would turn many of the children of Israel to the Lord their God. He would go before him in the spirit and power of Elijah, ‘to turn the hearts of the fathers to the children,’ and the disobedient to the wisdom of the just; to prepare a people prepared for the Lord.
SUMMARY: An angel assures Zacharias that his wife Elizabeth will bear a son named John, who will be filled with the Holy Spirit and prepare a people for the Lord.
