TITLE: Matthew 1:1-12 — The Genealogy of Jesus Christ
PROMPT: The book of the genealogy of Jesus Christ, the son of David, the son of Abraham. From Abraham came Isaac. From Isaac came Jacob. From Jacob came Judah and his brothers. From Judah came Perez and Zerah by Tamar. From Perez came Hezron. From Hezron came Ram. From Ram came Amminadab. From Amminadab came Nahshon. From Nahshon came Salmon. From Salmon came Boaz by Rahab. From Boaz came Obed by Ruth. From Obed came Jesse. From Jesse came King David. From David the king came Solomon by her who had been Uriah’s wife. From Solomon came Rehoboam. From Rehoboam came Abijah. From Abijah came Asa. From Asa came Jehoshaphat. From Jehoshaphat came Joram. From Joram came Uzziah. From Uzziah came Jotham. From Jotham came Ahaz. From Ahaz came Hezekiah. From Hezekiah came Manasseh. From Manasseh came Amon. From Amon came Josiah. From Josiah came Jechoniah and his brothers at the time of the exile to Babylon. After the exile to Babylon, from Jechoniah came Shealtiel. From Shealtiel came Zerubbabel.
SUMMARY: A list of ancestors tracing from Abraham to Zerubbabel is given as the beginning of Jesus' genealogy.
