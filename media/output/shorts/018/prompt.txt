TITLE: Mark 1:45-2:11 — A Paralytic’s Faith and Jesus’ Authority to Forgive
PROMPT: A man who had been healed by Jesus could not keep it to himself. He went out and began to proclaim what had happened, spreading the news everywhere. As a result, Jesus could no longer enter any city openly and stayed in deserted places. People came to him from everywhere. Some days later, Jesus returned to Capernaum, and word spread that he was at home. Soon, many gathered, leaving no room, not even around the door. As Jesus spoke the word to them, four people arrived carrying a paralyzed man. They could not get near him because of the crowd. So they opened the roof above Jesus. After breaking through it, they lowered the mat on which the man was lying. Seeing their faith, Jesus said to the paralytic, “Son, your sins are forgiven you.” Some scribes were sitting there, reasoning in their hearts. They thought, “Why does this man speak blasphemies like that? Who can forgive sins but God alone?” Jesus immediately perceived in his spirit that they were reasoning this way within themselves. He said to them, “Why do you reason these things in your hearts? Which is easier, to tell the paralytic, ‘Your sins are forgiven;’ or to say, ‘Arise, and take up your bed, and walk’? But that you may know that the Son of Man has authority on earth to forgive sins”—he said to the paralytic—“I tell you, arise, take up your mat, and go to your house.”
SUMMARY: A healed man spreads the news about Jesus, who later returns to Capernaum and forgives a paralytic’s sins, declaring his authority to forgive.
