TITLE: Matthew 2:16-23 — The Massacre of the Innocents and the Flight to Nazareth
PROMPT: When Herod realized the wise men had outwitted him, he was filled with rage. He sent out soldiers and ordered the killing of every male child in Bethlehem and throughout the surrounding countryside who was two years old or younger—according to the exact time he had learned from the wise men. Then, what had been spoken long ago by Jeremiah the prophet came true: “A voice was heard in Ramah, lamentation, weeping and great mourning, Rachel weeping for her children; she wouldn’t be comforted, because they are no more.” But when Herod died, an angel of the Lord appeared to Joseph in a dream in Egypt. The angel said, “Arise and take the young child and his mother, and go into the land of Israel, for those who sought the young child’s life are dead.” Joseph obeyed. He took the young child and his mother and went to the land of Israel. But when he heard that Archelaus was ruling in Judea in place of Herod, he was afraid to go there. Then, being warned in a dream, he withdrew into the region of Galilee and settled in a city called Nazareth. This happened so that what had been spoken by the prophets might be fulfilled: “He will be called a Nazarene.”
SUMMARY: Herod orders the massacre in Bethlehem, while God guides Joseph to bring Jesus and Mary safely to Nazareth.
